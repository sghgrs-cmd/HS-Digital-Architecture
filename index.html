Engineering & Clinical Standards

Measured speed, preserved SEO, and verified clinical polish.

Every clinic build follows strict engineering guardrails underneath the bespoke aesthetic — cleanly coded, search-safe, and verified before review.

Fast mobile first impression built to convert.

Built to target 90+ on Google PageSpeed & WCAG 2.1 AA across mobile and desktop using lightweight semantic code — placing doctor credibility, patient proof, and booking pathways in the opening mobile screen.

02 · 301 SEO & URL Preservation

Zero search-equity loss at cutover.

We catalog your clinical service pages, doctor profiles, and legacy blog or utility links, implementing clean 1-to-1 301 redirects so patients and search engines never encounter a broken URL.

03 · Dual-Layer QA Verification

Automated checks plus dedicated human QA.

Every page clears automated technical audits for contrast, layout, speed, and broken links, followed by a dedicated human QA specialist inspecting real phone and desktop screens before you ever open staging.
