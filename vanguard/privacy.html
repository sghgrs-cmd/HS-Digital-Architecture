Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Governs how Vanguard Bespoke Motors LLC collects, safeguards, and deletes client contact details submitted through our concierge platform. Effective August 2026.

Sections

1. General Provisions 2. Information Collected 3. Purpose of Processing 4. Legal Basis & Consent 5. Handover Photography 6. Storage & Security 7. Your Privacy Rights 8. Contact Information
01

General Provisions

This Policy defines the standards by which Vanguard Bespoke Motors LLC (hereinafter referred to as the Operator) handles personal contact information submitted via our website and private sourcing inquiries. By submitting a vehicle request, you acknowledge and accept the practices outlined below.

02

Information We Collect

When you submit a sourcing or consultation request, the Operator processes only:

  • Full name or preferred salutation
  • Direct telephone number and country code
  • Email address (when provided for written spec sheets)
  • Target vehicle specification, trim notes, and delivery state

We never collect Social Security numbers, driver’s license scans, residential gate codes, or wire/card credentials through public website forms.

03

Purpose of Processing

Your contact details are used strictly to prepare verified vehicle allocations, calculate all-inclusive turnkey customs and enclosed freight quotes, and coordinate showroom or residential handover. We never sell, rent, or broker client lists to third-party dealerships or ad networks.

04

Legal Basis & Consent

Information submitted through our concierge forms is processed solely on the basis of your explicit affirmative consent, recorded when you check the privacy confirmation box prior to transmission.

05

Client Handover Photography

Delivery photographs displayed in our Handover Gallery are published strictly with separate client permission obtained on delivery day.

If you appear in any gallery photograph and would like it removed at any time, email our Scottsdale concierge desk and we will permanently delete the image within 48 business hours with no questions asked.

06

Storage & Data Security

Contact records are retained only for the duration required to fulfill your vehicle inquiry or statutory commercial recordkeeping obligations, after which they are purged or anonymized. Technical and administrative controls protect all records against unauthorized access or disclosure.

07

Your Privacy Rights

  • Request a complete copy of the contact details we hold on file
  • Request immediate correction, restriction, or permanent deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to concierge communications at any moment
08

Contact Information

For any privacy inquiry or deletion request, email our compliance desk at concierge@vanguardmotors.com or call our Scottsdale office during business hours.
