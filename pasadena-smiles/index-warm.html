01 · Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Niloufar Molayem

DDS · Part-time professor · USC School of Dentistry

Dr. Niloufar Molayem practices holistic dentistry: no toxic materials, including the mercury in old silver fillings, and biocompatibility testing before any implant, filling or crown. She also trains future dentists as a part-time professor at the USC School of Dentistry.

She practices alongside Dr. Sanchita Mehra, a board-certified periodontist and implant specialist. Together they place SDS ceramic implants from Swiss Dental Solutions: metal-free zirconia that, where suitable, goes in during the same visit as the extraction.

We believe in treating our patients as a whole person, as conservatively as possible, to protect the natural tooth structure.
